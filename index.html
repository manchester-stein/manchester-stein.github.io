ABOUT THE WORKSHOP

Four days of talks and discussion

The workshop will take place at the Department of Mathematics, the University of Manchester, from 9 to 12 February 2027. It will bring together researchers working on Stein's method and its statistical applications.

Participants are welcome to contribute a poster to our informal lunchtime display. Details will be available under Registration & Posters.
