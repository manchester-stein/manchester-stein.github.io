Registration

Registration is free but compulsory for catering purposes.

Funding is available to support UK research students and early career researchers. Funding is also available for caring costs.

To apply for funding or enquire about registration, please contact the organising committee at manchester-stein@manchester.ac.uk.

SHARE YOUR RESEARCH

Lunchtime poster display

We invite you to share a poster at the workshop. Posters will be displayed throughout lunchtime outside the venue, where lunch will be served, giving participants something to browse while eating, resting and chatting.

There are no scheduled poster presentations. Authors are welcome to stand by their posters for informal discussions, but are not expected to be there at a set time. Any research topic is welcome, even if only remotely related to Stein’s method.
